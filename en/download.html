Before you download

Is my device compatible?

Tricolab runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. The optional one-time "Tricolab Complete" purchase (CA$4.99) unlocks every pattern and motif, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. No account, no ads, no trackers: your projects stay on your device.

Which languages?

Fully in English and French, with an FR / EN button in the app. Every pattern exists in both languages.

What about Android?

Not at the moment: Tricolab is for iPhone and iPad only.

How do I get help?

Email us at jdgeg@icloud.com or visit the Contact page.
